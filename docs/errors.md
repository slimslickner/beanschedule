## E001

**Error:** Schedule {id} is missing required metadata: "{key}".

Every schedule transaction needs its required metadata — `id` and `rrule` — and the metadata an amortized schedule needs. Add the named key, or remove the schedule.


## E002

**Error:** Schedule {id} has an invalid rrule: "{detail}".

`rrule` must be an RFC 5545 recurrence rule without a `DTSTART` part, e.g. `FREQ=MONTHLY;BYMONTHDAY=15`. The series is anchored by the transaction's date, not by the rule text.


## E003

**Error:** Schedule {id} is defined more than once.

A schedule id names exactly one transaction. Rename one of them, or remove it.


## E006

**Error:** Schedule {id} has an invalid method: "{mode}".

Choose `static` or `balance-from-ledger`. The `static` method needs `rate` and `term`; `balance-from-ledger` needs `rate` and the `principal` leg.


## E008

**Error:** Schedule {id} uses currency {currency}, but the operating currency is {operating_currency}.

Schedules support one currency: the ledger's `operating_currency`. Re-denominate the schedule, or set the correct option.


## E009

**Error:** Schedule {id} ends {end}, before it starts {start}.

The `end` of a series must be on or after the schedule's date. Swap or fix the dates, or remove `end` for an open-ended series.


## E011

**Error:** Schedule {id}'s postings total {total}, not {expected}.

The other-side postings of an occurrence must sum to the negative of the matched posting, so the transaction balances. Fix a posting's amount, drop one, or leave exactly one posting without an amount for Beancount to balance.


## E012

**Error:** Schedule {id} is amortized but has no `principal` leg.

An amortized schedule marks its legs with `schedule-leg`. Exactly one posting must carry `schedule-leg: "principal"` — the liability account the principal reduces.


## E014

**Error:** Schedule {id} leaves more than one posting without an amount.

Beancount can balance a transaction with at most one posting whose amount is missing. Give every posting but one an explicit amount.


## W001

**Warning:** Schedule {id} has {count} unresolved occurrence(s) past due, the oldest {date}.

A nominal occurrence has passed with no matching transaction. Import the missing transactions, reconcile, or skip the occurrence.
