## E001

**Error:** Schedule {id} is missing required metadata: "{key}".

Every `new` directive needs all of its required metadata. Add the named key, or remove the directive.


## E002

**Error:** Schedule {id} has an invalid rrule: "{detail}".

`rrule` must be an RFC 5545 recurrence rule without a `DTSTART` part, e.g. `FREQ=MONTHLY;BYMONTHDAY=15`. The series is anchored by `start`, not by the rule text.


## E003

**Error:** Schedule {id} is defined more than once.

A schedule id names exactly one rule. Rename one of the `new` directives, or remove it.


## E004

**Error:** "{verb}" names an unknown schedule "{id}".

A `skip` or `move` must name a schedule that a `new` directive defines. Check the spelling, or declare the schedule.


## E005

**Error:** "{verb}" for {id} on {date} is not on the rule's sequence.

A `skip` or `move` is dated at the nominal occurrence it resolves, so its date must be one the rule generates. Move it to a real occurrence date, or fix the rule.


## E006

**Error:** Schedule {id} has an invalid amount-mode: "{mode}".

Choose `exact`, `approx`, `range` or `any`. The `approx` mode needs `amount-tolerance`; the `range` mode needs `amount-min` and `amount-max`; `any` takes no amount hint.


## E008

**Error:** Schedule {id} uses currency {currency}, but the operating currency is {operating_currency}.

Schedules support one currency: the ledger's `operating_currency`. Re-denominate the schedule, or set the correct option.


## E009

**Error:** Schedule {id} ends {end}, before it starts {start}.

The `end` of a series must be on or after its `start`. Swap or fix the dates, or remove `end` for an open-ended series.


## E010

**Error:** A posting names an unknown schedule "{id}".

Every `custom "schedule" "posting"` must name a schedule that a `new` directive defines. Check the spelling, or declare the schedule. A posting with no rule is orphaned and is ignored.


## E011

**Error:** Schedule {id}'s postings total {total}, not {expected}.

The other-side postings of an occurrence must sum to the negative of `match-amount`, so the transaction balances. Fix a posting's `amount`, drop one, or leave exactly one posting without an amount for Beancount to balance.


## E012

**Error:** Schedule {id} mixes amortization with explicit postings.

An amortized schedule derives its principal, interest and escrow split from `rate`/`term`/`balance-from-ledger` and its `principal-account`/`interest-account`/`escrow-account`. It cannot also carry `custom "schedule" "posting"` directives. Remove one.


## E013

**Error:** A posting for {id} posts to its matched account {account}.

A posting's account is the other side of the occurrence; the matched account is `match-account` and carries the imported amount. Declare a different account, or drop the posting.


## E014

**Error:** Schedule {id} leaves more than one posting without an amount.

Beancount can balance a transaction with at most one posting whose amount is missing. Give every posting but one an explicit `amount`.


## W001

**Warning:** Schedule {id} has {count} unresolved occurrence(s) past due, the oldest {date}.

A nominal occurrence has passed with neither a matching transaction, a `skip`, nor a `move`. Import the missing transactions, or record a `skip` if it will never arrive.


## W002

**Warning:** "{verb}" for {id} on {date} is outside the series window.

The skip or move resolves a nominal date that the rule does not generate within `start` and `end`, so it has no effect. Move it onto a real occurrence, or extend the series.
